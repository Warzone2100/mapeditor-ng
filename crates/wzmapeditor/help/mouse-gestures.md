## Mouse & Modifier Gestures

These are fixed interactions built into the tools rather than rebindable actions, so they are the same on every install.

- `Ctrl + click` — eyedropper: samples the tile, orientation, height, or ground type under the cursor.
- `Shift + click` — arms line-draw on the Texture, Ground type, and Height brush **Set** tools.
- `Right-click` — cancels a line draw or stamp capture. Hold it to rotate or fly the camera.
- `Ctrl + drag` — box-selects vertices in Vertex Sculpt. Add `Shift` to extend the existing selection.
- `Shift + scroll` — adjusts the camera move speed. Holding the right button instead of `Shift` does the same. Scrolling with neither held has no effect.

## Rebindable keys

Everything else is a configurable action. Open Settings and choose `Keybindings` from the sidebar to see the current bindings and change them: click the button next to any action, then press the combination you want (optionally with `Ctrl`, `Shift`, or `Alt`) and it saves immediately. Press `Esc` alone to clear a binding and leave the action unbound, or click **Reset All to Defaults** to start over.

If two actions share the same key, a conflict warning appears at the top of that list.
