## Creating your first map

Click `New` on the toolbar (or `File > New Map...`) to open the New Map dialog. Set the width and height, give your map a name, pick a tileset (Arizona, Urban, or Rockies), and choose an initial height for the terrain. Click `Create` and the editor loads a blank map ready for editing.

To open an existing map, click `Open…` on the toolbar (or `File > Open .wz...`) and choose a `.wz` archive. You can also drag and drop a `.wz` file directly onto the editor window.

On desktop, `Map Browser` on the toolbar (or `File > Browse Maps...`) lists the maps already installed with your copy of Warzone 2100, so you can pick one without hunting for its file. It is disabled in the web build.

See [Creating & Opening Maps](maps-create.md) for details on importing legacy formats and browsing your local map collection.

## A quick tour of the window

The editor's main window is a dockable layout built around a central 3D viewport where you see and interact with your map. To the left is a column of terrain tools and a tileset tile picker. Around the viewport are panels for Assets, Selection, Minimap, Hierarchy, Problems, Output, and Balance — each can be repositioned or resized. If the layout looks wrong, `View > Reset Layout` restores the default arrangement.

For a full breakdown of every panel and control, see [Interface Overview](interface-overview.md).

## Getting help

- Press `F1` anywhere in the editor to open a help topic relevant to what you are currently doing.
- Click the `?` in the corner of a panel for that panel's topic.
- Open `Help > Help Topics…` to browse all topics.
